# Lost, Delayed, and Damaged Baggage Procedures

## 1. Immediate Reporting (Critical Protocol)
- **Do Not Exit:** Passengers missing baggage MUST NOT exit through the Customs red/green channels. Once you exit the secure baggage hall, liability claims may be invalidated.
- **Action:** Proceed immediately to the Airline Baggage Service Desk located between Belts 3 and 4 in the Arrivals Hall.

## 2. Filing a Property Irregularity Report (PIR)
To file a formal PIR, the passenger must provide:
- The physical or digital **Boarding Pass**.
- The **Baggage Claim Tags** (the small barcode stickers issued at check-in).
- A detailed description of the bag (Brand, color, dimensions, hard/soft shell, distinct markings/ribbons).
*Note: The system will generate a 10-character WorldTracer Reference Number (e.g., BOMAB12345). Retain this number.*

## 3. Tracking and Delivery
- Passengers can track their PIR status live via `https://worldtracer.aero` using their Reference Number and Last Name.
- Once located, the airline will arrange complimentary courier delivery to the address provided on the PIR within a 50km radius of the airport.

## 4. Compensation Guidelines
- **Delayed Bags (Over 24 Hours):** Passengers traveling away from their home city are entitled to a daily "Essential Items" reimbursement (keep all receipts for toiletries/underwear).
- **Lost Bags (Over 21 Days):** A bag is officially declared "Lost" after 21 days. Compensation claims must then be filed directly with the airline's central customer service portal under the Montreal Convention limits.
