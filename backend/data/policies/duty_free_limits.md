# Comprehensive Duty-Free & Customs Allowances Policy

## 1. Alcohol & Tobacco Limits
Allowances apply strictly to passengers aged 18 and older. Allowances cannot be pooled among multiple passengers.
- **Alcoholic Beverages:** - Maximum of 2 liters of spirits (liquor) OR 2 liters of wine.
  - Must be carried in a Secure Tamper-Evident Bag (STEB) with the receipt visibly sealed inside.
- **Tobacco Products:** - Maximum of 200 cigarettes, OR 50 cigars, OR 250 grams of loose smoking tobacco.
  - E-cigarette liquids containing nicotine are subject to the same volume limits as standard LAGs (Liquids, Aerosols, and Gels) and must be declared.

## 2. Currency & Monetary Instruments
Failure to declare monetary instruments may result in confiscation and legal penalties.
- **Inbound Declaration:** Any passenger carrying currency, traveler's checks, or bearer bonds exceeding $10,000 USD (or foreign equivalent) must file a formal declaration at the Customs Red Channel before exiting the baggage hall.
- **Outbound Declaration:** Passengers exporting more than $5,000 USD in physical currency must declare it at the outbound customs desk prior to security screening.

## 3. High-Value Goods & Electronics
- **Personal Electronics:** Laptops, cameras, and personal devices intended for personal use do not require declaration.
- **Commercial Goods:** New items in sealed packaging exceeding a total combined value of $500 USD must be declared for import duties.

## 4. Strictly Prohibited Imports
The following items will be seized immediately by customs enforcement:
- **Food & Agriculture:** Fresh fruits, vegetables, seeds, unpasteurized dairy, and raw meat products.
- **Regulated Items:** Counterfeit consumer goods (fake designer wear), unlicensed pharmaceuticals, and wildlife products (ivory, specific furs).
- **Media:** Obscene materials or seditious literature.
