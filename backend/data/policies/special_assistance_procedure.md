# Special Assistance (PRM) & Wheelchair Protocols

## 1. Passenger with Reduced Mobility (PRM) Categories
Assistance is categorized by international aviation codes. Passengers must specify their code when booking:
- **WCHR (Wheelchair Ramp):** Passenger can walk up stairs and to their seat but requires a wheelchair for long terminal distances.
- **WCHS (Wheelchair Steps):** Passenger cannot climb stairs but can walk to their seat.
- **WCHC (Wheelchair Cabin):** Passenger is completely immobile and requires an aisle chair and physical lift to their seat.

## 2. Booking Timelines & Requirements
- **Pre-Booking:** All PRM requests must be registered with the airline a minimum of **48 hours** prior to the scheduled departure time.
- **Medical Clearance:** Passengers requiring oxygen (WCHC) or traveling post-surgery must carry a signed "Fit to Fly" medical certificate dated within 7 days of the flight.

## 3. Day-of-Travel Protocol
- **Arrival Point:** Proceed directly to the **Special Assistance Hub** located at Door 3 in the Landside Arrivals Hall. Do NOT wait in standard check-in lines.
- **Check-in:** A dedicated Special Assistance agent will handle check-in, baggage drop, and priority security screening.
- **Personal Wheelchairs:** Battery-powered wheelchairs MUST have dry/gel-cell batteries. The battery must be disconnected and the terminals insulated before it is stowed in the aircraft hold.

## 4. Unaccompanied Minors (UMNR)
- Applies to children aged 5 to 14 traveling alone.
- The parent/guardian must present government-issued photo ID at the check-in desk and remain at the airport until the flight has physically taken off.
