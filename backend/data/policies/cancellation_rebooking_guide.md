# Flight Disruption, Cancellation, & Compensation Guide

## 1. Division of Responsibility
- **Airport Authority:** Responsible for terminal safety, seating, announcements, and facility operations. *Airport staff cannot rebook tickets or issue refunds.*
- **Airline Operators:** Responsible for all rebooking, compensation, and voucher issuance.

## 2. Entitlements for Delays (Airline Controlled)
If a flight is delayed due to airline fault (crew availability, technical faults):
- **2+ Hour Delay:** Entitled to telecommunication access (free Wi-Fi) and a minor refreshment voucher.
- **4+ Hour Delay:** Entitled to a hot meal voucher redeemable at designated airside food courts (e.g., McDonald's, Costa Coffee).
- **Overnight Delay (8+ Hours):** Entitled to a hotel accommodation voucher and complimentary shuttle transfers to/from the hotel.

## 3. Disruption Rebooking Process
1. Wait for an official SMS or app notification from your airline. Many airlines auto-rebook passengers on the next available flight digitally.
2. If manual rebooking is required, proceed to the specific Airline Transfer Desk located in the Airside Central Plaza.
3. Do NOT exit the secure airside zone unless explicitly instructed to do so by the airline for a hotel transfer, as you will have to reclear security.
