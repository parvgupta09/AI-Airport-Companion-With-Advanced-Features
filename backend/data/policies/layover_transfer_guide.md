# Comprehensive Layover & Transit Transfer Protocol

## 1. Baggage Transfer Policies (Through-Checked vs. Self-Transfer)
Baggage handling during a layover is determined by ticket booking type. Passengers must verify their baggage tag destination.
- **Single Itinerary (Through-Checked):** If all flights are booked under a single PNR (ticket), checked baggage is automatically transferred to the final destination. Passengers **MUST NOT** exit to the Landside Baggage Claim.
- **Separate Tickets (Self-Transfer):** If flights are booked on separate itineraries, baggage is "short-checked." Passengers **MUST** clear immigration, collect baggage from the Landside belts, proceed to the T1/T2 Check-in Counters to re-check the bag, and reclear security.
- **International to Domestic Exception:** Regardless of ticket type, passengers arriving on an international flight connecting to a domestic flight must collect their baggage at the first port of entry, clear Customs, and drop the bag at the dedicated "Transit Baggage Drop" desk.

## 2. Airside Transit (Sterile Area Connections)
For passengers with a through-checked bag and an onward boarding pass who do not need to leave the secure zone.
- **Inter-Terminal Transfer (T1 to T2):** Passengers must follow the purple "Flight Connections" signage to the Airside Central Plaza. Board the **Airside Transit Train** (runs every 3 minutes, 24/7). Do not exit through the glass security doors.
- **Transit Security Checkpoint:** All transit passengers must pass through a mandatory Airside Transit Security X-ray before entering the departure concourse of their next flight. 
- **LAGs (Liquids) Exception:** Duty-free liquids purchased on the first flight are permitted through Transit Security ONLY IF they remain sealed in an official STEB (Secure Tamper-Evident Bag) with the receipt visible.

## 3. Landside Transfer (Immigration & Terminal Change)
For passengers who must exit the sterile area (self-transfers, re-checking bags, or lacking an onward boarding pass).
- **Visa Requirements:** Exiting the Airside zone requires passing through Immigration. Passengers must hold a valid tourist or transit visa for the layover country.
- **Inter-Terminal Shuttle Bus:** After collecting bags and clearing Customs, passengers must exit to the Landside curbside. The complimentary **Landside Shuttle Bus** departs from Door 4 (T1) and Door 2 (T2) every 10 minutes.
- **Re-Entry:** Passengers must clear the main Departure Security Checkpoints in their onward terminal. 

## 4. Minimum Connection Time (MCT) Compliance
The automated e-gates and security systems enforce strict timeframes for connections.
- **Domestic to Domestic (D-D):** Minimum 45 minutes connection time.
- **International to International (I-I):** Minimum 90 minutes connection time.
- **International to Domestic (I-D):** Minimum 120 minutes connection time (accounts for mandatory Customs clearance).
- **Gate Arrival Requirement:** Regardless of layover length, passengers must be physically present at their onward boarding gate exactly **30 minutes** prior to departure.

## 5. Missed Connection Protocols
- **Airline Fault (Single Ticket):** If the inbound flight was delayed causing a missed connection, the passenger must proceed directly to the **Airline Transfer Desk** in the Airside Central Plaza. The airline is legally bound to rebook the passenger on the next available flight at no cost.
- **Passenger Fault / Separate Tickets:** If the passenger booked separate tickets and misses the connection (due to inbound delays or slow self-transfer), the onward airline holds zero liability. The passenger is classified as a "No-Show" and must purchase a new ticket at the Landside Ticketing Desk.
- **Overnight Layovers (>8 hours):** Passengers with airline-fault delays exceeding 8 hours are entitled to a hotel voucher. They must be escorted Landside by airline staff.
