# Comprehensive Baggage & Prohibited Items Policy

## 1. Carry-On (Hand Luggage) - Prohibited Items
The following items are strictly prohibited in the cabin and will be confiscated at security:
- **Sharp Objects:** Knives (any blade length), box cutters, scissors (blades > 6cm), razor blades (unless in a cartridge), needles, and swords.
- **Tools:** Hammers, screwdrivers, wrenches, drills, and crowbars.
- **Sporting Goods:** Baseball bats, cricket bats, golf clubs, and hockey sticks.
- **Explosives/Flammables:** Fireworks, blasting caps, aerosol spray paint, and fuel.
- **Self-Defense Items:** Pepper spray, mace, brass knuckles, and stun guns.

## 2. Special Electronics Policy (Cabin Only)
- **Lithium Batteries & Power Banks:** Must be carried in **hand luggage only**.
  - **Limit:** Max 2 power banks per passenger.
  - **Rating:** Must not exceed 100Wh (Watt-hours). Batteries between 100Wh-160Wh require explicit airline approval.
  - **Prohibition:** Damaged, deformed, or unbranded/homemade power banks are strictly forbidden.
- **E-Cigarettes/Vapes:** Must be carried in hand luggage. Use or charging inside the terminal or aircraft is strictly prohibited.
- **Lighters:** Only one lighter per passenger is allowed, and it must be carried on your person (pocket), not in the hand luggage bag itself. Torch lighters are prohibited.

## 3. Liquids, Aerosols, and Gels (LAGs) Policy
- **Container Size:** Every container must be 100ml (3.4 oz) or less.
- **The "One-Bag" Rule:** All items must fit into one transparent, resealable 1-liter plastic bag (approx. 20cm x 20cm).
- **Exceptions:**
  - Medicines (liquid/gel) required for the flight (with valid prescription).
  - Baby food/milk required for the duration of the flight.
  - Duty-free liquids purchased in tamper-evident bags.

## 4. Checked Baggage - Restricted Items
Items allowed in checked luggage but NOT in hand luggage:
- **Tools:** Larger tools exceeding 6cm.
- **Liquids:** Full-sized toiletries, alcoholic beverages (subject to airline limits).
- **Replicas:** Toy guns, realistic-looking props, or martial arts weapons.

## 5. Medical & Mobility Equipment
- **Medication:** Always carry in original packaging with the prescription/doctor's note.
- **Inhalers:** Allowed in cabin (up to 2).
- **Mobility Aids:** Canes, crutches, and walkers are subject to security screening. Collapsible wheelchairs will be stowed in the aircraft hold at the gate.
