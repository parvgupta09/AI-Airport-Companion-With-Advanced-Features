# Official Airport Wi-Fi Connectivity Protocol

## 1. Standard Free Connection (4-Hour Access)
- **Step 1:** Enable Wi-Fi on your device and select the SSID: `MegaHub_Free_WiFi`.
- **Step 2:** A captive portal will launch automatically. If it does not, open a browser and navigate to `http://connect.megahub.local`.
- **Step 3:** Select "Passenger Login".
- **Step 4:** Enter your exact **Flight Number** (e.g., AB123) and the **Last Name** exactly as it appears on your boarding pass.
- **Step 5:** Enter a valid mobile number with the country code. A 6-digit One-Time Password (OTP) will be sent via SMS.
- **Step 6:** Input the OTP within 5 minutes to activate your 4-hour session.

## 2. Premium Wi-Fi (Unlimited/High-Speed)
- Available for Business/First Class passengers and paid subscribers.
- Connect to SSID: `MegaHub_Premium`.
- Log in using the 8-character alphanumeric Lounge Voucher Code provided at the check-in desk or lounge reception.

## 3. Troubleshooting & Kiosk Assistance
- **No SMS Received:** If your carrier blocks international SMS, proceed to any blue "Information & Connectivity Desk" (located in Landside and Airside Plazas).
- **Passport Scanning:** Insert your passport into the desk's physical scanner to instantly print a paper receipt containing a temporary Wi-Fi access code.
- **MAC Randomization:** Devices using "Private Wi-Fi Addresses" or MAC randomization may experience session drops. Disable this feature in your device network settings for stable connectivity.
